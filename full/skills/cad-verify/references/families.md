# Check families

Owner: cad-verify. One row per registered family
([cad-record registered check families](../../cad-record/references/contract-index.md#registered-check-families), 54 families): the anchor
that names its obligations, whether `hw/check-plan.yaml` carries it, who produces the result, and the method owner. Result shape, outcome
codes and facets: [results.md](results.md). Scope items: [check-plan.md — Expected scope](check-plan.md#expected-scope).

- **Plan** `plan`: the check plan carries the check, criterion and cases. `fixed`: no check-plan entry; the check id is the family with `.`
  and `_` replaced by `-` (`geometry.identity` → `geometry-identity`), except `drawing.coverage` → `drawing-<subject in lowercase>` and
  `release.integrity` → `package-integrity`.
- **Producer**: `lead` ([cad-flow stages — run-checks](../../cad-flow/references/stages.md#run-checks)), `checker` (`cad-checker` unit),
  `reviewer` (`cad-mech-dfm-reviewer` unit). A method owned outside cad-verify is run exactly as its owner states; this table adds only the
  anchor and the outcome rule.
- Every row: a method that cannot run, an input that is missing or a criterion that is UNKNOWN gives UNKNOWN with the matching code, never
  PASS.
- **Facet**: a `plan` family takes the check-plan facet. A `fixed` family records `CAD_VALID`; its outcome counts toward a requirement facet
  in the verification index only when it names that requirement.

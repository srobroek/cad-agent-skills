# Check families

Owner: cad-verify. One row per registered family
([cad-record registered check families](../../cad-record/references/contract-index.md#registered-check-families), 54 families): the anchor
that names its obligations, whether `hw/check-plan.yaml` carries it, who produces the result, and the method owner. Result shape, outcome
codes and facets: [results.md](results.md). Scope items: [check-plan.md — Expected scope](check-plan.md#expected-scope).

- **Plan** `plan`: the check plan carries the check, criterion and cases. `fixed`: no check-plan entry, except a lite build's criterion
  mapping ([check-plan — Lite build](check-plan.md#lite-build)); the check id is the family with `.` and `_` replaced by `-`
  (`geometry.identity` → `geometry-identity`), except `drawing.coverage` → `drawing-<subject in lowercase>` and `release.integrity` →
  `package-integrity`.
- **Producer**: `lead` ([cad-flow stages — run-checks](../../cad-flow/references/stages.md#run-checks)), `checker` (`cad-checker` unit),
  `reviewer` (`cad-mech-dfm-reviewer` unit). A family stopped at acceptance (run-checks step 4), or whose unit was lost, stalled or
  rejected and not re-dispatched ([cad-flow delegation — lost and stalled units](../../cad-flow/references/delegation.md#lost-and-stalled-units)
  step 3), gets a `lead` result whose outcomes are all UNKNOWN `NOT_RUN`, or `TIMEOUT` for a stalled unit, whatever its Producer. A
  method owned outside cad-verify is run exactly as its owner states; this table adds only the anchor and the outcome rule.
- Every row: a method that cannot run, an input that is missing or a criterion that is UNKNOWN gives UNKNOWN with the matching code, never
  PASS.
- **Facet**: a `plan` family takes the check-plan facet. A `fixed` family records `CAD_VALID`; its outcome counts toward a requirement facet
  in the verification index only when it names that requirement or, in a lite build, when a criterion mapping assigns that requirement's
  criterion to it.

## Lite build

A lite build is a product whose `hw/product.yaml` `build_mode` is KNOWN `LITE`
([cad-record record — Build mode](../../cad-record/references/record.md#build-mode)). It runs only the families its stage policy lists
under `lite_stages` ([cad-flow stages.yaml](../../cad-flow/assets/stages.yaml)), each FORBIDDEN to waive; a full-only family gives no
obligation, result or verdict entry. Anchor, producer and FAIL rule are the rows above; a lite method below replaces only the Method
column, and only in a lite build.

| Stage | Family and anchor | Lite method | PASS only when |
|---|---|---|---|
| GROUND, LAYOUT, RELEASE | `record.schema`, `record.semantic`, `record.ownership`, `record.inputs` `@product`; `delivery.integrity@product` when the attempt dispatched a unit | the rows above, on the record shapes cad-record selects by build mode | as above; no producer's technical result counts as independent evidence |
| GROUND | `intake.use_case@product` | [cad-select — Choose the build mode](../../cad-select/references/selection.md#choose-the-build-mode) and [Lite intake](../../cad-select/references/selection.md#lite-intake); a domain lite never asks may be absent | those headings hold; UNKNOWN `REQUIRED_UNKNOWN` when `build_mode` is UNKNOWN |
| GROUND | `requirements.completeness@product`, `check_plan.completeness@product` | [check-plan — Lite build](check-plan.md#lite-build) | per that heading |
| LAYOUT | `intake.use_case@product` | [Lite intent inspection](#lite-intent-inspection), a checker REVIEW | per that heading |
| LAYOUT | `geometry.build`, `geometry.readback`, `geometry.export` `@assembly` and `@<PN>`; `geometry.identity@<PN>.<component>`; `preview.render` `@assembly` and `@<PN>` | the Lite paragraph of each [cad-model exports](../../cad-model/references/exports.md#lite-build) heading; `geometry.readback` includes [FreeCAD readback](../../cad-model/references/exports.md#freecad-readback) | per those headings; FreeCAD absent is UNKNOWN `NOT_RUN`, never PASS |
| LAYOUT | `geometry.interference@assembly` when the assembly has more than one geometric occurrence | [geometry.interference](geometry-motion.md#geometryinterference) step 1 over all n(n−1)/2 occurrence pairs, repeated placements included | every pair shares at most 1e-3 mm³; no fit excuses an overlap. With one occurrence the family gives no obligation, never a zero-pair PASS |
| LAYOUT | `geometry.clearance@assembly` when a requirement states a geometric clearance limit | [geometry.clearance](geometry-motion.md#geometryclearance) step 1 at the named static pose | every sourced limit holds; other pairs are reported ungraded; no fit or tolerance claim |
| RELEASE | `verification.index@product` | the row above, over the index [check-plan — Lite build](check-plan.md#lite-build) derives from accepted LAYOUT results | no row, outcome or cited hash differs, no criterion or scope gap, no UNKNOWN upgraded |
| RELEASE | `release.inventory@product`; after approval `release.integrity@product` | the rows above | per those headings |

Lead results in a lite build are the rows above whose Producer is `lead`. Lead diagnostics in scratch never replace a checker result,
even when deterministic.

### Lite intent inspection

`intake.use_case@product` at LAYOUT of a lite build: a lite-only method run by the `cad-checker` with a REVIEW implementation
([results — Implementation kind](results.md#implementation-kind)).

1. Read, and list in `inspected` by `hw/` path with its `input_inventory.files` sha256: the snapshot `product.yaml`, `requirements.yaml`,
   `check-plan.yaml` and `assembly.yaml`, each PN's `part.yaml` and `model.py`, and the version's `previews/assembly.svg` and
   `previews/parts/<PN>.svg`. Never another check's pending result.
2. Compare the frozen intended form (the product description, each requirement's text and criteria, the part count and arrangement) with
   what each `model.py` builds and the previews show: shape, part and occurrence count, static pose, and every requested feature (an opening,
   wall, bore, round, lip) on its named part.
3. Account every requirement criterion: it has exactly one check-plan entry due LAYOUT whose family measures it. Every number stays the
   COMMAND readback's; this inspection judges presence and form only.
4. Write one outcome, facet `CAD_VALID`, with this heading as its POLICY `criterion`, a `value` text naming each matched statement and the
   model and preview files as `evidence`.

PASS when every statement matches and every criterion is accounted. FAIL when a requested feature, part or occurrence is missing, the form
differs from a statement, a feature or part the owner did not ask for changes the form, or a criterion is unaccounted; each FAIL has a
finding naming the statement, the file and what it shows. UNKNOWN `REQUIRED_UNKNOWN` when the record leaves a statement's meaning open.
Never NOT_APPLICABLE. Limitations: form against stated intent only; no dimension, fit, strength or manufacturing claim, and never the
owner's approval.
